# Projects

A shelf of the work — current pieces first, then the smaller tools that stay in service.

## ShellBeacon Telemetry

An APRS beacon in pure shell script for a Raspberry Pi: position, station telemetry, and patio weather to APRS-IS every twenty minutes, with optional RF transmission on 144.390 MHz and a spoken voice announcement on 146.430 — no radio required, the Pi transmits itself. A fork of WA1GOV's shellbeacon, extended by KJ5GBY.

[The project](/shellbeacon/) · [Live station page](https://www.cenzontle.us/kj5gby/) · [Source](https://github.com/robertmcatee/shellbeacon)

## The Roll of Arms of Bexar

The living register of the College of Arms of Bexar — *Registrum Armorum San Antonio de Béxar*. Arms borne in and around San Antonio, Texas: the State, the County, the City, its military, its churches and institutions, and its people, each entered with its blazon.

[The Roll](/bexarcollegeofarms/) · [Source](https://github.com/robertmcatee/bexarcollegeofarms)

## Cozy Cats: Cat Sort Puzzle

A paid Android puzzle game — sort the cats, cozy up. In production on Google Play since October 2026.

[About the game](/cozycats/) · [Source](https://github.com/robertmcatee/cozycats)

## The Kajukenbo Family Tree
