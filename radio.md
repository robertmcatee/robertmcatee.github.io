# Radio

I'm Robert McAtee, KJ5GBY, in San Antonio, Texas — grid EL09.

## Where you'll find me

Mostly on **146.430 MHz FM simplex**. No repeater, no network, no
account — just VHF, the way local radio is supposed to work, and
the one channel everybody actually has a radio for. I monitor it
at home, and my station announces there too: if you hear a calm
synthesized voice give the time and the patio temperature, that's
my beacon. Key up and say hello — the human is usually in the
next room.

The digital modes are in the toolbox and I do use them, they're
just niche by comparison: DMR talkgroups 310217 and 31482, the
AA5RO repeater system, and 40 metres CW when the band is open.

TG 310217 is tied to XLX410A, the San Antonio area reflector — a
single place for local repeaters and remote users to stay in
touch whatever their digital mode. D-Star and Fusion users hear
it too, so please don't quick-key; leave a breath between
transmissions.

## APRS — the home station

KJ5GBY is also an automated station: a Raspberry Pi Zero W in the
house running ShellBeacon, an open-source APRS beacon I maintain
(forked from WA1GOV's shellbeacon). Every 20 minutes it reports
position and station telemetry, plus live outdoor weather decoded
by an RTL-SDR from a patio sensor — on APRS-IS and simultaneously
on 144.390 MHz RF — and then speaks its ID, the time, and the
temperature on 146.430.

ShellBeacon is listed on the [APRS Foundation's New Developments
page](https://how.aprs.works/new-developments/).
Code: [shellbeacon-telemetry on GitHub](https://github.com/robertmcatee/shellbeacon-telemetry)
